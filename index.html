How We Operate

Buy the operations, not the story.

Most of the value in a small multifamily building is unlocked after closing — in tenant quality, expense control, and the discipline to fix what previous ownership let slide. That is where we spend our time.

01
Underwrite what exists, not what's promised.
We buy on in-place income and conservative expense assumptions. Upside from repositioning is the reward for doing the work — it is never what makes the deal pencil.
02
Fix the building before the numbers.
Tenant screening, deferred maintenance, and vendor relationships come first. Stabilized operations are what make a refinance possible — Mount Royal and Westlynn both followed that order.
03
Be reachable. Report plainly.
Investors and owners deal with Kevin directly. No investor-relations layer, no quarterly gloss — the same numbers we manage to are the numbers you see.
Where We Invest

Discipline over geography.

We underwrite to fundamentals — rent-to-price ratios that still work at today's rates, stable employment bases, and buildings small enough to manage hands-on. Right now that points to Edmonton and Saskatoon. It will point elsewhere when the numbers do.
