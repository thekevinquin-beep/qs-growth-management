Overview

A turnaround, not a gut renovation.

Mount Royal is the full cycle in one building: a troubled acquisition, an operational reset, and a refinance that returned capital inside twelve months.

Acquired in March 2025 with Kevin as an equity partner. The property came with a history of crime and problem tenancies that had suppressed both rents and the building's reputation in the neighbourhood. Under new ownership it was repositioned into a stable, family-oriented building — through tenant turnover and screening, not a capital-intensive renovation.

The refinance delivered a 30% return to investors, with approximately $600K in value added within the first year. Kevin holds an equity position; day-to-day asset management is handled by the operating partner in Saskatoon.

Timeline

Twelve months, start to refi.

  1. March 2025

    Acquisition

    Purchased with a local operating partner. Underwritten on in-place income with the reputation problem priced in — not on a projected turnaround.

  2. Months 1–8

    Reposition the tenancy

    Problem tenancies resolved, a consistent screening standard applied on every turnover, and the building re-established as a safe, family-oriented address.

  3. Year one

    Stabilize and refinance

    With operations stable and income proven, the property was refinanced — returning 30% to investors and crystallizing roughly $600K in added value.

Field Note
The building didn't need new kitchens. It needed the right tenants and a landlord who would answer the phone. Everything in the return traces back to that.
— K.Q.S.
