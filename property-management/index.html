How We Manage

Property management focused on growing your property's value.

I manage rental properties in London, Ontario, from single-family homes up to apartment buildings, using the same approach I take as an investor and asset manager. Every decision I make is about maximizing your property's value, decreasing expenses and increasing income.

What I Do

Full-service management.

Everything a rental needs, handled in one place.

  1. 01

    Tenant placement

    I handle the listing, showings, tenant screening and the lease itself. You get a tenant who has been vetted before they move in.

  2. 02

    Rent collection

    Rent gets collected, and late payments get followed up rather than left to slide. You hear about a problem early instead of after a month has already slipped.

  3. 03

    Maintenance and repairs

    Repairs are coordinated with trades so issues get handled instead of sitting. I keep you informed on what's being done and what it costs.

  4. 04

    Owner reporting

    You get reporting on income and expenses, so you always know where the money is coming from and where it's going.

  5. 05

    Full-service management

    Full-service management covers the rest of the day-to-day work, so you don't have to pick up the pieces yourself.

The Asset Manager Lens

Income, expenses, value.

The same three questions I ask when I invest in a property myself.

01
Income
I look at where your rent sits against the market and what each lease decision does to your cash flow. Rent isn't just what the last tenant paid. It's a number to test against what the market supports.
02
Expenses
I review the expense lines on your property and look for costs that can come down without cutting corners. I also flag deferred maintenance, since putting off a repair usually costs more later.
03
Value
Every decision gets weighed against what it does to the property's value over time. It's the same question I ask when I invest in a property myself.
